What it will cover

Scope: finite elements and PDE solvers.

Nothing here is released. This is the scope of the plan, as PLAN.md states it: item 13 of wave 3 (go-geometry meshing and go-fem: P1/P2 elements, Poisson, heat, elasticity, validated by manufactured solutions).

△

Lagrange elements

P1 and P2 elements on triangles, with tabulated symmetric quadrature rules of high degree and positive weights.

∑

Assembly and boundary conditions

An element, dofmap and assembler split after the FEniCS book, with Dirichlet, Neumann and periodic conditions.

∇²

Poisson first

Then heat, wave and elasticity, and Stokes later: the MATLAB PDE Toolbox and NDSolve surface is far larger, and the plan says so.

✓

Manufactured solutions

Pick the exact solution, derive the right-hand side and the boundary data, and report the observed convergence order on a refinement sequence.

🔗

Built on the fleet

Meshes from go-geometry, linear solvers from go-sparse, stiff time stepping from go-numerics: nothing is rewritten.

⚖️

Licences kept clean

MFEM (BSD-3-Clause) is the design reference; FEniCS and DOLFINx (LGPL) are oracles only.
